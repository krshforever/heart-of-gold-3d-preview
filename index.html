Room IV • Custom Commission

The 6-Step Bespoke Atelier

From loose stone curation to final hallmarking, every bespoke commission is personally guided by our master karigars.

01
Private Consultation

One-on-one session at our GK-1 salon or virtually to define occasion, silhouette, and heirloom intent.

02
Gemstone Curation

Hand-selection of certified Zambian emeralds, Burmese rubies, and uncut Polki parcels.

03
Gouache & 3D CAD

Traditional hand-painted gouache rendering paired with precision 3D proportion modeling.

04
Wax & Fit Trial

Ergonomic neck-curve and ring-balance trial before gold casting begins.

05
Karigar Goldsmithing

Hand-forged 22K gold articulation, kundan setting, and reverse meenakari detailing.

06
Certification & Handover

BIS hallmarking, independent lab certification, and velvet vault presentation.
